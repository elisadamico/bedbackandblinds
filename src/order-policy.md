<p><em>Last updated October 29, 2026.</em> This policy applies to every order placed on our website. By checking out, you agree to it. Questions before you order? Call us at <a href="{{ site.phone_href }}">{{ site.phone }}</a>.</p>

<h2>Cancelling an order</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Within 24 hours, before we place it with the manufacturer:</strong> full refund.</li>
<li><strong>After we have ordered it from the manufacturer:</strong> we can only cancel if the manufacturer accepts it back. If they do, a 25% restocking fee applies. If they don't, the order can't be cancelled.</li>
<li><strong>Once your order has shipped, been delivered, or been picked up:</strong> it can't be cancelled. See returns below.</li>
<li><strong>Custom window treatments</strong> are made to fit your windows and can't be cancelled or returned once ordered.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Returns</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Mattresses, pillows, bedding, and protectors</strong> can't be returned or exchanged once delivered, picked up, or opened, for health and hygiene reasons. Where a manufacturer offers its own comfort exchange or trial, it is handled under that manufacturer's terms, and we'll help you with it.</li>
<li><strong>Furniture</strong> can't be returned once delivered or picked up, except for defects covered under warranty.</li>
<li><strong>Clearance, floor models, and items sold as-is</strong> are final sale.</li>
<li><strong>TEMPUR-Cloud® mattresses shipped by UPS</strong> come straight from Tempur-Pedic. Any trial or return is under Tempur-Pedic's terms. Please call us before starting one.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Damage and defects</h2>
<ul>
<li>Please inspect your order when it's delivered or picked up, and note any visible damage at that time.</li>
<li>Tell us about any damage within 48 hours, with photos. We'll arrange a repair or replacement through the manufacturer.</li>
<li>Products are covered by the manufacturer's warranty. Bed Back & Blinds doesn't add a separate warranty.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Delivery</h2>
<ul>
<li>We deliver within 45 miles of our Dubuque store. The delivery fee is based on the ZIP code you enter in the cart. If your delivery address is in a different ZIP code, we may adjust the fee or cancel the order with a full refund.</li>
<li>We'll call you to schedule delivery. Please make sure someone 18 or older is home and the path is clear.</li>
<li>Please measure your doors, stairways, and hallways before you order. We aren't responsible if an item doesn't fit, and it can't be returned for that reason.</li>
<li>If a delivery can't be completed because no one is home or the item can't be brought in safely, a redelivery fee equal to the original delivery fee applies.</li>
<li>Delivery dates depend on the manufacturer and are estimates, not guarantees.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Store pickup</h2>
<ul>
<li>We'll call you when your order is ready. Please bring your order confirmation and a vehicle that fits your items.</li>
<li>Orders not picked up within 30 days of our call may be cancelled, with a 25% restocking fee taken from the refund.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Prices and availability</h2>
<ul>
<li>We work hard to keep prices and stock up to date. If a price is listed in error or an item is no longer available, we may cancel the order and refund you in full.</li>
<li>Sale prices apply only during the dates of the sale.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Refunds</h2>
<ul>
<li>Refunds go back to the card you paid with, usually within 10 business days of approval.</li>
<li>If there's a problem with your order, please call us first so we can make it right.</li>
</ul>
