Bed Back &amp; Blinds is a family owned business with over 35 years experience. We offer the best quality products with competitive pricing on Tempur-Pedic and Serta mattresses and mattress sets including the Tempurpedic Cloud, Tempurpedic Contour and the newest state of the art Tempurpedic Flex hybrid and Tempurpedic Breeze, Serta iComfort, Serta iComfort Hybrid, Serta iSeries, Serta Perfect Sleeper and iAmerica by Serta mattresses.

We can send your bed in a box with various gel memory foam beds, gel toppers, latex pillows, feather pillows, down pillows, memory foam pillows and position pillows. We carry Tempur-Pedic adjustable beds, Serta adjustable beds and Legett &amp; Platt adjustable beds. We also sell bedgear hi tech cooling mattress and pillow protectors. back sleeper, side sleeper and stomach sleeper pillows, and cooling sheets. We also offer linens and furniture accents from Ollixx. Plus, more quality furniture and accessories from Coast to Coast Accents Furniture, Coaster Furniture, Catnapper power lift chairs, Windermere lift chairs, Jackson Furniture sectionals, sofas, love seats, chairs as well as Fashion Bed Group headboards and platform beds, day beds and futons.

Bed Back and Blinds also offers free in-home window decorating consultations carrying high end brands such as Hunter Douglas, Alta Window Fashions, Norman Window Fashions, Graber, Paris Texas Hardware, Kirsch and Carole Fabrics. Our designer quality companies carry the most current and up to date selections such as cordless and remote controlled wood blinds, faux wood blinds, roller shades, honeycomb shades, tri-light shades, woven wood, roman shades, window shadings, vertical blinds, Silhouette, Luminette, Pirouette, Vignette and Architella energy efficient window coverings.

Our decorator will assist you with custom drapery, sheers, valances, bedding and pillows with a wide assortment of current, trendy, transitional and classic fabrics. Custom window decorating is made affordable at Bed Back &amp; Blinds.
