Bed Back &amp; Blinds is a family owned business with over 35 years experience. We offer the best quality products with competitive pricing on Tempur-Pedic and Sealy mattresses and mattress sets, including the TEMPUR-Adapt®, TEMPUR-ProAdapt®, and TEMPUR-LuxeAdapt® collections and Sealy® Golden Elegance and Remington mattresses. We also carry Tempur-Pedic adjustable bases, including the TEMPUR-Ergo® Smart Base.

For a complete night's sleep, we carry Malouf pillows, sheets, and mattress protectors, and our furniture selection comes from Coaster Fine Furniture.

Bed Back and Blinds also offers free in-home window decorating consultations carrying high end brands such as Hunter Douglas, Alta Window Fashions, Norman Window Fashions, Graber, Paris Texas Hardware, Kirsch and Carole Fabrics. Our designer quality companies carry the most current and up to date selections such as cordless and remote controlled wood blinds, faux wood blinds, roller shades, honeycomb shades, tri-light shades, woven wood, roman shades, window shadings, vertical blinds, Silhouette, Luminette, Pirouette, Vignette and Architella energy efficient window coverings.

Our decorator will assist you with custom drapery, sheers, valances, bedding and pillows with a wide assortment of current, trendy, transitional and classic fabrics. Custom window decorating is made affordable at Bed Back &amp; Blinds.
